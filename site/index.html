macOS · PlateWatch Hub

One console for reviewed camera feeds.

Watch moving HLS video with tracked vehicle boxes, or browse a clustered camera map. Browse, search and sort cameras. Pin favorites, block unavailable views, and open a selected snapshot in Live to inspect its analysis overlays. Search, filters, sort, selection and scroll remain in place when you switch sections. Records & FOIA drafts and tracks fleet requests, reviews court/list candidates, and links records through cited public-capacity profiles. Published-source links include NYC prosecutor credibility releases and Arizona investigative reporting; one-click list import currently covers Washington certification actions. Background work continues outside Live. Frames stay in memory; fleet observations pass the same privacy gates.

Agent tools: a local authenticated MCP interface lets Codex, Claude and OpenCode inspect feeds and records, set stars or blocks, open Live, review saved evidence and draft fleet FOIA text. CSV imports remain pending for review. Setup and internal-agent roadmap. In-app Apple Foundation Models and shared-store MLX agents are implemented; PCC remains planned. Hosted research is explicitly selected and excludes local Review evidence.

Camera catalogues include reviewed snapshot providers and keyed 511 sources. Imports add metadata, and new hosts stay inactive until terms review. Access keys, provider availability and image quality vary; a catalogue entry is not proof of a working stream. See the development branch's source matrix.

How it works

Everything sensitive happens on the device. Only structured, human-reviewable detections that clear a fleet-confidence gate ever sync.

Camera→Vehicle & plate detect→OCR + multi-frame fusion→Fleet-confidence gate→Outbox→CloudKit / REST mirror→Public dataset
ON-DEVICE

Apple Neural Engine

Vision text recognition with optional installed CoreML detectors. Analysis stays on the device.

GATED

Fleet-only ingestion

A RedactionGate drops private-plate reads before upload; exact location is withheld until a vehicle is fleet-confirmed.

SOURCED

Provenance on everything

Enrichment — agency, unit, cost — carries a citation to a public record. No citation, no enrichment.

OPEN

Portable by design

CloudKit for Apple devices, a Vapor REST reference server for everyone else, AGPL-3.0 throughout.

Non-negotiable principles

Encoded in the data layer, not just the docs.

Watches the powerful, never the privatePublic officials in their public capacity are fair subject matter — recording them on duty is protected in many US jurisdictions, though the rule varies by circuit and state. Private individuals, their plates and their homes are out of scope absolutely.
Public records onlyFleet registries, FOIA releases, rosters and auction listings. Never DMV or data-broker data.
On-device firstNo telemetry, analytics SDKs, frame uploads or third-party trackers.
Evidence qualityEvery sighting is content-hashed and timestamped, with its sources.

Roadmap

Current focus is M-Alpha: make the core loop work end to end. Full list in the roadmap.

FIRST · RELIABILITY

Finish the pilot

  • Sustained feed freshness, CPU and memory checks
  • Measure tracking on the same saved clips
  • Resolve remote app/UI CI failures
  • Field and spoken accessibility acceptance
MODELS · M2

Improve measured recognition

  • Plate-state model: more data, calibration and real-photo validation
  • Fleet classifier on vehicle crops
  • Evaluate make/model and vehicle re-ID
  • Specialized OCR only if it beats Vision
WORKFLOWS · M4/M7

Complete the records loop

  • Cited intake and duplicate checks
  • Proposed vehicle/unit-to-record links
  • Human review and publication acceptance
  • CloudKit two-account isolation
DEPLOYMENT · M5

Exercise the reference clients

  • Hosted REST mirror
  • Android and edge runtime QA
  • Physical watch push delivery
  • CarPlay entitlement and device test
