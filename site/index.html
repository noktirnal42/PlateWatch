Watch the public fleet.

PlateWatch is an open-source, on-device license plate recognition platform for one purpose: holding taxpayer-funded vehicles — police, fire, transit, public works, federal fleets — accountable. Private-citizen plates are redacted by design.

How it works

Everything sensitive happens on the device. Only structured, human-reviewable detections that clear a fleet-confidence gate ever sync.

Camera→Vehicle & plate detect→OCR + multi-frame fusion→Fleet-confidence gate→Outbox→CloudKit / REST mirror→Public dataset
ON-DEVICE

Apple Neural Engine

Vision text recognition today, CoreML detectors and classifiers next. No frame has to leave the phone.

GATED

Fleet-only ingestion

A RedactionGate drops private-plate reads before upload; exact location is withheld until a vehicle is fleet-confirmed.

SOURCED

Provenance on everything

Enrichment — agency, unit, cost — carries a citation to a public record. No citation, no enrichment.

OPEN

Portable by design

CloudKit for Apple devices, a Vapor REST reference server for everyone else, AGPL-3.0 throughout.

Non-negotiable principles

Encoded in the data layer, not just the docs.

Government and fleet focusThe platform tracks publicly funded assets. It is not a people-tracking tool.
Public records onlyFleet registries, FOIA releases, rosters and auction listings. Never DMV or data-broker data.
On-device firstNo telemetry, analytics SDKs, frame uploads or third-party trackers.
Evidence qualityEvery sighting is content-hashed and timestamped, with its sources.
